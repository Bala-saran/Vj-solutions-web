# Asset credits
## Video backgrounds: NOT INCLUDED
The brief asked for real, licensed black-and-white stock footage (Pexels, Pixabay, Coverr). The build environment cannot reach those sites (outbound network is restricted), so no video files were downloaded and none are used. Nothing was AI-generated and no external video URLs are hotlinked.
To add them: download clips you are licensed to use, convert to a muted, looping, black-and-white MP4/WebM (e.g. `ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -an -vf "hue=s=0,scale=1280:-2" -crf 30 out.mp4`), add a poster image, place them in public/video/, and record each source URL and licence below.
